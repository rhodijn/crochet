We bring together digital cultural collections and the timeless art of crochet to turn inspiration into something tangible.

Our idea

What if digital objects could become handmade creations?

Digital collections hold thousands of fascinating objects, each with its own shape, story and character. But what if we could experience these objects beyond a screen?

masche. explores this idea by connecting open cultural data with the creative world of crochet. Users can discover objects from digital collections, select something that inspires them and transform it into a crochet project with generated instructions.

Our goal is to make digital collections more interactive, accessible and inspiring through the art of making.

How it works

From data to stitches.

01 — Discover

Browse objects from an open data collection and find something that inspires you.

02 — Personalize

Select your preferred crochet style, materials, size and skill level.

03 — Generate

Transform your selected object into personalized crochet instructions.

04 — Create

Follow the instructions and bring your own interpretation to life.
